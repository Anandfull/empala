Why Lithium Battery

Built to deliver more energy, for longer

Lithium-ion chemistry offers clear advantages over traditional lead-acid and NiCd systems — lighter weight, deeper usable capacity and significantly longer service life.

High Energy Density

More usable energy per kilogram and per litre, so packs stay compact and lightweight. Typical cell-level values range from roughly 150–250 Wh/kg depending on chemistry.

Long Cycle Life

LiFePO₄ configurations can be specified for several thousand charge–discharge cycles at defined depth of discharge, reducing total cost of ownership over the system's life.

Fast Charging

Lithium cells accept higher charge currents than lead-acid equivalents. With correct thermal design and BMS limits, packs can be charged at elevated C-rates safely.

Low Maintenance

No water topping-up, no equalisation charging and no memory effect. Sealed construction keeps service requirements minimal in most installations.

High Efficiency

Round-trip energy efficiency is typically very high, meaning less energy is wasted as heat during charging and discharging compared with older battery chemistries.

Eco-Friendly Technology

Lithium packs contain no cadmium and support cleaner renewable integration. We operate a take-back and end-of-life recycling programme for used battery packs.

Battery Technology

From cell to complete battery system

Every LithoPower pack is engineered as a layered system. Each level adds protection, structure and intelligence — so the finished pack performs predictably in real conditions.

01

Cell

Individual electrochemical unit

02

Module

Cells grouped with interconnects

03

Battery Pack + BMS

Managed, protected, ready to deploy

We work with proven lithium-ion chemistries including LiFePO₄ (lithium iron phosphate) for maximum cycle life and thermal stability, and NMC (nickel manganese cobalt) where higher energy density is the priority. Chemistry selection is matched to your duty cycle, temperature range and space constraints.

Cells are the core building block. We source from qualified manufacturers and screen incoming cells for capacity, internal resistance and self-discharge, so only matched cells are assembled into the same module. This grading step is critical to balanced, long-lasting packs.

Cells are grouped into modules with spot-welded or laser-welded busbars, insulation barriers and structural framing. Modules are the serviceable unit — if a module degrades, it can be replaced without discarding the entire pack.

Modules are assembled into a finished pack with an enclosure rated to the installation environment, main contactors, fusing, service disconnects, communication interfaces and mounting points. Enclosure design accounts for IP rating, vibration and impact requirements.

The BMS is the brain of the pack. It monitors individual cell voltages, pack current and temperature, performs cell balancing, calculates state of charge and state of health, and enforces safe operating limits. Communication options include CAN, RS485 and UART for integration with inverters, vehicles and monitoring platforms.

Temperature directly affects performance and service life. Packs can be specified with natural convection, forced-air cooling or liquid cooling depending on the power profile. Temperature sensors placed across the pack feed the BMS, which derates or disconnects the load if limits are approached.

Lithium packs require a charger matched to the chemistry and voltage. Charging follows a constant-current / constant-voltage profile, with the BMS controlling the final balance stage. Using an incompatible or unregulated charger can reduce life and is not covered under warranty.

Safety & Protection

Multiple layers of protection, engineered in

Lithium batteries are safe when designed, installed and used correctly. Our packs combine electronic, thermal and mechanical safeguards. No battery system can be described as absolutely risk-free — correct installation and use remain essential.

Battery Management System

Continuous monitoring of cell voltage, current, temperature and state of charge, with automatic derating or shutdown when limits are exceeded.

Overcharge Protection

Charge is cut off at the configured maximum cell voltage, preventing excessive lithium plating and the heat build-up associated with overcharging.

Over-discharge Protection

The BMS disconnects the load before cell voltage drops into a range that would permanently reduce capacity and cycle life.

Short-Circuit Protection

Fusing, contactors and electronic current limiting respond within milliseconds to isolate fault conditions and limit fault energy.

Temperature Monitoring

Multiple NTC sensors across the pack feed real-time temperature data to the BMS, which reduces current or stops operation if thresholds are reached.

Cell Balancing

Active or passive balancing keeps cells at consistent voltage, maximising usable capacity and preventing weak cells from limiting the whole pack.

Important note: Lithium battery systems must be installed and maintained by qualified personnel using compatible chargers and inverters.
